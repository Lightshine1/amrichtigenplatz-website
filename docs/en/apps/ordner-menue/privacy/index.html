Privacy Policy for “Ordner-Menü”

Last updated: November 2026

In short

Ordner-Menü does not collect any personal data and does not send anything over the internet. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking and no user account.

Data the app uses

The app reads the names of the folders in the start folder you choose (iCloud Drive by default) and its subfolders in order to show them in its window. It does not read file contents. This information never leaves your Mac.

Filing files

When you drag files onto the menu bar icon, the app moves or copies them to the folder you pick only after you confirm. Existing files are never overwritten.

What is stored

Your settings (start folder, shortcut, visible top-level folders, position) and the “Recently used” list with the paths of recently opened folders are stored locally on your Mac only. You can clear or switch off the list in the settings.

Download

The app is provided through this website. The information on hosting in our general privacy policy applies to downloads.

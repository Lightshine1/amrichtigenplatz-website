Battery Limit

Charge limit for your MacBook battery

Charging only to 80 or 90 % helps your battery last longer. Battery Limit sets the charge limit with one click in the menu bar and shows the battery level clearly inside its icon.

Download for free

Version 1.2 · 0.5 MB · MacBook with Apple silicon, macOS 26.4 or later

On Mac

About the app

A lithium battery ages more slowly when it isn't kept at 100 % all the time. Battery Limit uses the charge limit built into macOS and puts it right in your menu bar.

Charge limit

  • Charge to 80, 85, 90, 95 or 100 % – one click in the menu
  • macOS stores the limit itself, so it applies even when the app isn't running
  • "Charge to full once": go to 100 % before a trip, the limit returns after you unplug
  • If the battery is above the limit while plugged in, the Mac runs on the power adapter and the battery slowly drops to the limit

Menu bar

  • Battery percentage right inside the battery icon – you can hide Apple's own icon
  • The icon's outline shows the charge level, a small arrow marks the limit
  • Bolt while charging, plug when on power
  • Blinks gently when the battery is very low (adjustable)

Schedule

  • A separate charge limit for each day of the week
  • Optionally switch the evening before, so the battery is full in the morning

Notifications

  • When the charge limit is reached
  • When the battery is low
  • When the power adapter is too weak, or the battery drains despite being plugged in

Battery health

  • Condition as a percentage of the original capacity and number of charge cycles
  • Monthly history as a chart

Energy use per app

  • Which apps used the most energy in the last 6 or 24 hours – as a bar chart with the battery level
  • Top 10 with percentage; helper processes count towards their app, system services under "macOS/System"
  • Optionally only the time on battery
  • The measuring itself uses well below 0.1 % of the battery per day; the data stays on your Mac for 48 hours

Every feature can be turned on or off in the settings. Battery Limit needs no internet connection, collects no data and shows no ads.

Languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified).

Privacy Policy for “Battery Limit”

Last updated: November 2026

In short

Battery Limit does not collect any personal data and does not send anything over the internet. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking and no user account.

Data the app uses

The app reads the battery level, charging state, battery health, charge cycles and power adapter wattage directly from your Mac in order to display them and to set the charge limit. These values never leave your Mac.

What is stored

Your settings and – if you have turned on the history – one entry per month with battery health and charge cycles are stored locally on your Mac only. You can delete the history in the app at any time.

Notifications

Notifications are created locally on your Mac. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

Download

The app is provided through this website. The information on hosting in our general privacy policy applies to downloads.

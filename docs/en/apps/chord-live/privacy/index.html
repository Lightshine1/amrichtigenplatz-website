Privacy Policy for “Chord Live”

Last updated: November 2026

In short

Chord Live does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The music is analyzed exclusively on your device.

Microphone access

To recognize chords, the app listens to the music through the microphone. It asks for your permission on first launch; you can revoke access at any time in the Settings of your iPhone or iPad.

The audio signal is analyzed in memory only, within fractions of a second (pitches → chord). Nothing is recorded, nothing is stored and nothing is sent to any server. The app has no network features.

Settings

Your settings (e.g. level, instrument, sensitivity) are stored locally on your device only.

No analytics, no advertising

The app contains no analytics or advertising services, no tracking technologies and no third-party libraries.

Your rights

Since the app does not process any personal data, we hold no data about you from the app. If you email us, we use your details only to answer your request. Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (responsible for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Ansbach, Germany).

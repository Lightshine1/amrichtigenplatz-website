Chord Live

Real-time chord recognition

Music is playing and you want to join in, but there's no sheet music? Chord Live listens and shows the chords instantly in huge letters – with a preview of what's next.

About the app

Chord Live listens through the microphone and shows the chords of the music that's playing in real time – big, easy to read, on a black background. Perfect for jumping in and playing along, even without sheet music.

It's that simple

Put your iPhone or iPad next to you, let the music play – from the radio, a speaker or live – and play along. The current chord fills the whole screen, in portrait or landscape.

Four levels

  • Simple: major and minor only
  • Standard: adds 7, m7, maj7, sus2 and sus4
  • Extended: adds 6, m6, add9, diminished and augmented
  • Pro: every chord, including bass notes (e.g. C/E)

For guitar or piano

In guitar mode, Chord Live shows easy-to-play variants such as Bm7 instead of Bm or Fmaj7 instead of F. They fit the music and need no barre.

What's next?

The chords most likely to come next appear on the right – the brighter, the more likely. The prediction knows the harmony of the key and learns the song's progressions as it goes: when the chorus comes back, Chord Live knows what's coming.

Key

The detected key of the song is shown at the top right.

Thoughtful details

  • Talking and noise don't count as music
  • A new song starts automatically after a short pause
  • Optional German notation (H instead of B)
  • The screen stays on while the app is running

Private

The music is analyzed only on your device. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. No account, no ads, no data collection.

Chord Live is free.

Compress a PDF on iPhone and Mac – without an online service

PDF too big to upload or send? Here's how to compress PDFs on iPhone and Mac right on your device – text stays sharp and searchable.

Scanned contracts, bank statements or presentations with lots of photos quickly reach 20, 50 or more megabytes. But many upload forms – at insurers, government offices or job portals – only accept a few megabytes.

Why PDFs get so big

It's almost always the images inside the PDF: scans and photos are often embedded at full camera resolution. The actual text takes up hardly any space.

Be careful with online services

There are many websites that “compress PDFs for free”. To use them, you upload your document to someone else's server – not a good idea for contracts, payslips or medical letters. A solution that works right on your device is better.

How to do it with Resize Everything

  1. Tap “Choose from Files” and pick the PDF (on Mac: drag it into the window).
  2. The app shrinks the photos inside the PDF. Text and vector graphics stay untouched – sharp and searchable.
  3. If needed, keep only certain pages or save individual pages as JPEG, PNG or HEIC.
  4. The new size is shown at the bottom before you export.
  5. Tap “Export”, then upload, share or save it to Files.

The PDF never leaves your device.

Without an extra app (Mac)

On Mac, the Preview app can shrink PDFs via “Export” and the Quartz filter “Reduce File Size”. The result is often very blurry, though, and there's hardly anything to adjust. On iPhone there's no built-in option.

Video too big for WhatsApp? Here's how to send it anyway

Can't send a video on WhatsApp, or it arrives blurry? Here's how to trim and shrink videos on iPhone and Mac – and see the file size before you export.

A phone video of just a few minutes can easily be several hundred megabytes. WhatsApp shrinks such videos itself when sending – often so much that they arrive blurry. Very long videos sometimes can't be sent as a regular video at all.

It's better to prepare the video yourself first. Then you decide what matters: shorter and sharp beats long and pixelated.

Why videos are so big

Current iPhones record in 4K at 30 or 60 frames per second. That looks great on a big TV, but it's far more than a phone screen in a chat needs. For WhatsApp, 720p is usually plenty – about one ninth of the pixels of 4K.

The three most effective steps

  1. Trim: Cut the start and the end. Every second you remove saves space.
  2. Lower the resolution: 720p instead of 4K makes the file many times smaller – on a phone you'll hardly see the difference.
  3. Efficient format: MP4 with H.264 plays on every device. HEVC (H.265) is even smaller, but older Android phones can't always play it.

How to do it with Resize Everything

  1. Open the app and tap “Choose from Photos” (on Mac: just drag the video into the window).
  2. Set the start and end with the yellow handles.
  3. Tap “WhatsApp” – format, resolution and quality are set for you.
  4. At the bottom you immediately see roughly how big the video will be. Still too big? Trim more or lower the quality.
  5. Tap “Export”, then send it straight to WhatsApp via “Share / Save”.

Everything happens on your device – the video is never uploaded anywhere.

Without an extra app

In the Photos app you can trim a video via “Edit”. But you can't lower the resolution there – and you don't see how big the file will end up. For short clips that's sometimes enough; for long videos only shrinking helps.

In short

SSD Guard does not collect any personal data and does not send anything over the internet. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking and no user account.

Data the app uses

The app reads the free and total space of your startup disk in order to display it and to warn you. In the “Free Up Space” window it also determines how much space caches, log files, Xcode data, unopened iCloud Drive files and the Trash take up. These values never leave your Mac.

What is deleted or offloaded

The app only removes the areas you explicitly select in the “Free Up Space” window. When offloading from iCloud Drive, only the local copy is removed; the file stays in your iCloud account. The app empties the Trash via Finder, for which macOS asks for your permission once.

What is stored

Your settings (thresholds, check interval, display) are stored locally on your Mac only.

Notifications

Notifications are created locally on your Mac. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

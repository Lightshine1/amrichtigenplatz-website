In short

Resize Everything does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All files are processed exclusively on your device.

Processing your files

Videos, images and audio files you open in the app are read and converted only locally on your Mac, iPhone or iPad. Nothing is sent to any server. The app has no network features.

Temporary copies created during editing are kept in the app's private storage and are deleted by the system or the next time the app starts.

Access to Photos

On iPhone and iPad you pick photos and videos using the system picker; the app only sees the items you select. When you tap “Save to Photos”, the app asks for permission to add new items to your library. Read access to your library is never requested.

No analytics, no advertising

The app contains no analytics or advertising services, no tracking technologies and no third-party software.

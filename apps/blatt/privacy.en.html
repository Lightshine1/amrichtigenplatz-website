In short

Blatt does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your texts and spreadsheets belong to you and stay wherever you save them.

Your documents

Texts and spreadsheets are opened, edited and saved only locally on your Mac, iPhone or iPad – in the folder you choose. If you save them to iCloud Drive or another cloud storage service, the transfer is handled by the system or that provider; Blatt itself does not send any data to servers. The app has no network features of its own.

When you tap a link in a document, it opens in your browser.

Settings

Your settings (such as font sizes and text styles) are stored only on your device.

No analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics or advertising services, no tracking technologies and no third-party software.

Your rights

Because the app does not process personal data, we hold no data about you through the app. If you email us, we use your details only to answer your request. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (responsible for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Ansbach, Germany).

Blatt is a text editor and a spreadsheet in one tiny app. Only a few megabytes in size, it opens instantly and responds without delay – and it still has the most important features on board.

Blatt isn't trying to replace Word or Excel. It's for everything in between: notes, lists, letter drafts, a household budget, a shopping or packing list, or a quick calculation. No menu jungle, no account, no subscription.

TEXT
• Headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight
• Bulleted and numbered lists, plus checklists to tick off
• Quotes, links, dividers and code
• Table of contents with collapsible headings
• Saved as Markdown – readable in any program. The characters are hidden, so you see the result right away
• Export as PDF, Word, RTF, HTML, Markdown or plain text; Word, OpenDocument, RTF and Pages files can be opened
• Font sizes and spacing to your taste

SPREADSHEETS
• Formulas with around 90 functions such as SUM, IF, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS and COUNTIFS
• Real dates: type a date, count the days to a deadline, workdays and week numbers
• Clickable links to websites and email addresses
• Freeze rows and columns; color highlighting from Excel is kept
• Print and PDF with page numbers
• Font size, bold, italic, text color and background color for cells
• Sort, fill, one-tap sum, insert rows and columns
• Opens and saves Excel (.xlsx, .xltx), OpenDocument (.ods) and CSV, reads Numbers spreadsheets
• Export as PDF, HTML or Markdown

SMALL AND FAST
Blatt is deliberately lean. No ads, no data collection, no sign-in. Everything stays on your device or in your iCloud Drive.

On iPhone and iPad a format bar sits right above the keyboard; on the Mac everything is also available via keyboard shortcuts.
